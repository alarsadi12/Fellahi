Privacy policy

We respect your privacy. Most features in the current version of Fellahi run in your device's browser.

Data and use

  • Plant records, irrigation dates and language preferences may be stored locally in your browser.
  • The current version has no user accounts or server processing these records.
  • A plant photo is previewed in the page; it is not sent to an AI service by this feature.
  • If external services or ads are added, this policy will be updated before activation.
