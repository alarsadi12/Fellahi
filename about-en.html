About Fellahi

Fellahi is a simple assistant for farmers, bringing together basic tools and introductory information to help organize crop and tree care.

Current features

  • An approximate fertilizer calculator based on user-entered area and rate.
  • Local irrigation reminders and plant records.
  • An introductory guide to selected crops.
  • A form to prepare plant photos and symptoms; automated AI diagnosis is not currently available.
